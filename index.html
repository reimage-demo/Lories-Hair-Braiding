Before your visit.

Do I need an appointment?

We accept walk-ins, but prefer appointments. Micro twist, Sister Locks, and Sunday services are by appointment only. Call (860) 953-5838 to arrange your visit.

Do you take deposits?

We do not take deposits online. If a deposit is arranged with the salon, it is handled in person only. Please call Lorie’s directly with any payment questions.

Do I need to bring hair?

For micro braids, please buy and bring your own hair. For other styles, call us before your visit to confirm which hair and supplies you need.

Can I have my hair washed at the salon?

Yes. A wash and blow-dry on its own is $35. A hair wash when you are getting your hair braided here is $20.

Can I book for Sunday?

Yes, Sundays are by appointment only. An additional $30 applies. Call the salon to confirm a time before coming in.
