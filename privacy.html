Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy describes information handled through the Lorie’s African Hair Braiding & Beauty Salon website and when you contact the salon.

Information you share

This website does not provide a booking form, customer account, or online checkout. When you call or visit, you may share your name, contact details, appointment preferences, and information needed to discuss your service. The salon uses that information to respond to you and manage your appointment.

Website operation

The website’s hosting and security providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and the time of your visit to deliver and protect the website. We have not added advertising pixels or analytics scripts to this website.

Your browser remembers whether the introductory hero animation has played so it appears only on your first visit. This setting stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing this site’s browser data resets it.

TikTok and external links

TikTok videos load here only after you select a “Watch video” button. “View photo post” and “Open on TikTok” links open TikTok in a new tab. Loading a video or opening a TikTok link connects your browser to TikTok. TikTok may receive technical and usage information and use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy. You can browse the rest of this site without loading those posts.

Links to TikTok, Google Maps, and REIMAGE BUSINESS SOLUTIONS take you to independently operated websites. Their privacy practices and terms apply there. Read TikTok’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy for more information.

Use and sharing

Information you provide to the salon is used to answer questions, coordinate services, and handle related business needs. Service providers may process information to operate the website or support the salon. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Your questions and choices

You can avoid loading embedded posts, manage cookies through your browser, and contact the salon to ask about information you have shared or request a correction or deletion. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law. Please do not send payment details through social media.
